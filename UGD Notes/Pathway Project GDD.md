## Overview
### Working Title
Normal Putt
### Pitch
A wacky physics based Mini-Putt game, where the player can influence the ball after being shot.

## Core Design Elements
### Player Goal
To complete the course in as few strokes as possible.

### Rules and Mechanics
Use anything you can, from spin to obstacles and power ups to get your shot in!

### Challenges & Obstacles
Course design and dynamic mini golf obstacles to present a challenge.
